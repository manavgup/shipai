About Ship AI

Ship AI is a podcast exploring AI trends, enterprise adoption, and what it means for business leaders and technologists.

Your Host

Manav Gupta brings decades of experience in enterprise technology to unpack the most important trends in artificial intelligence. Each episode combines deep research with practical insights for leaders navigating the AI transformation.

The Show

Ship AI covers the full spectrum of AI's impact — from the economics driving billion-dollar investments to the workforce implications reshaping every industry. The show features data-driven analysis, interactive visualizations, and conversations with practitioners building AI at scale.

Current Series

  • State of AI 2025 — A 6-part deep dive into the BOND AI Trends Report covering adoption speed, economics, global competition, workforce impact, and what's next.
  • Guest Conversations — Interviews with AI leaders, practitioners, and researchers shaping the future of enterprise AI.
