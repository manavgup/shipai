Your Host

Meet Manav Gupta

Manav Gupta brings decades of experience in enterprise technology to help leaders navigate the AI transformation. With a background spanning global consulting, product strategy, and hands-on AI implementation, he understands both the promise and the practical realities of deploying AI at scale.

Throughout his career, Manav has worked at the intersection of technology and business — helping organizations turn complex technical capabilities into real competitive advantage. He has seen multiple technology waves firsthand, from cloud computing to big data to the current AI revolution.

With Ship AI, Manav brings this depth of experience to a wider audience — translating dense research reports, earnings calls, and technical breakthroughs into clear, actionable insights for the people making decisions about AI adoption today.
