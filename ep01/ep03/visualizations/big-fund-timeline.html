Strategic Shift: From Fabs to Chokepoints
Unlike previous phases focused on scaling fabrication capacity, Phase III targets supply chain chokepoints — the technologies where China remains dependent on foreign suppliers. Lithography, EDA software, photoresists, specialty gases, and High-Bandwidth Memory are the new battlegrounds. The goal: ensure Chinese fabs cannot be choked off by foreign restrictions.
