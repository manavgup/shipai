The GPU-Poor Paradigm
Denied access to NVIDIA's H100 GPUs by US export controls, DeepSeek innovated "up the stack" — developing Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture and Multi-Head Latent Attention (MLA) that dramatically reduce compute requirements. The $5.6M figure represents GPU pre-training costs only; total infrastructure investment is estimated at ~$1.3B including R&D and servers.
