The Chokepoint Strategy
US export controls target 4 critical chokepoints (marked with !) where China lacks alternatives: advanced GPUs, HBM memory, leading-edge fabrication, and EDA software. China's response through Big Fund III specifically targets these gaps, but closing them requires 5-10 years of sustained investment. Meanwhile, China leads in algorithmic efficiency — DeepSeek's MoE architecture achieves comparable results with 18x less compute cost.
