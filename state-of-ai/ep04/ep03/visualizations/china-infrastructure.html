The Energy Arbitrage Strategy
Domestic Chinese chips (Huawei Ascend 910B) have lower performance-per-watt than NVIDIA H100s. By locating AI training in western provinces with cheap renewable energy (solar, wind, hydro), China effectively subsidizes the efficiency gap. What would cost $X in coastal data centers costs $0.5X in Gansu or Inner Mongolia. This transforms a hardware disadvantage into an infrastructure advantage.
