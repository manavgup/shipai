State-Led vs Market-Driven
As Western private capital floods into AI, China's VC ecosystem is transitioning to state-led funding. Big Fund III's $47.5B dwarfs any single VC fund, but represents a fundamentally different model: strategic semiconductor sovereignty vs market-driven innovation. China's Q4 2024 dropped to just $5.8B, down from $10.3B in Q3 — a 44% quarterly decline.
