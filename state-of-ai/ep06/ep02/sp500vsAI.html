📈 How to Pull This Data

  • Yahoo Finance API (yfinance): Free Python library for real-time stock data
  • Bloomberg Terminal: Most accurate institutional data ($24k/year)
  • Alpha Vantage: Free API with rate limits, good for basic analysis
  • S&P Dow Jones Indices: Official index methodology and weights
  • FRED (Federal Reserve): Economic data including market indices

🔧 Calculation Methodology

  • Get S&P 500 index total return for the period (^GSPC)
  • Calculate individual stock returns for the 7 AI companies
  • Determine market cap weights (company market cap ÷ total S&P market cap)
  • Calculate contribution: weight × stock return × correlation factor
  • Sum contributions to get total AI impact percentage
  • S&P return without AI = Total return × (1 - AI contribution)
⚠️
Market Concentration Risk: This extreme concentration means the entire market's health depends on just 7 companies. Combined with the circular funding patterns where these companies invest in each other, this creates systemic risk. If the AI bubble bursts, there's little underlying growth to cushion the fall.
